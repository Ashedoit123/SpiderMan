# 🕷️ Spider-Man: Web-Slinger AR Game

An interactive web-based superhero game powered by **MediaPipe Hand Tracking**, real-time webcam computer vision, dynamic Verlet web physics, and procedural audio synthesis.

---

## 🤟 How to Play with Hand Tracking

1. **Click "START WEBCAM"** on launch (make sure to grant camera permissions).
2. Raise your hand in front of your camera to act as **Spider-Man**!
3. **The Iconic Spider-Man Web Shooting Gesture (🤟)**:
   - **Thumb**: Extended / Open
   - **Index Finger**: Extended / Pointing straight
   - **Middle Finger**: Curled / Folded into palm
   - **Ring Finger**: Curled / Folded into palm
   - **Pinky Finger**: Extended / Straight
4. **Special Gestures**:
   - **🤟 "THWIP!" Web Blast**: Fires high-speed web projectiles and attaches elastic braided web ropes!
   - **✊ Fist (Power Pull / Yank)**: When an object or enemy is webbed, clench your fist or pull your arm back to yank them across the screen!
   - **🖐️ Open Hand (Web Shield / Spread)**: Spreads a burst of web nets to catch multiple incoming hazards.
   - **Dual-Wielding**: Both left and right hands can shoot webs independently or simultaneously!

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 1. 🕸️ Free Play (Spider-Man Sandbox)
- **Zero pressure, pure superhero freedom!**
- Shoot webs anywhere on screen to create procedural 8-spoke web splat decals.
- Interact with floating **Oscorp Drones**, bouncing **Green Goblin Pumpkin Bombs**, wooden **Crates**, toxic **Barrels**, and swinging **Target Bulls-eyes**.
- Web objects and swing your hands to whip them around and smash them together!
- Floating action toolbar lets you spawn more objects, trigger web bombs, clear webs, or reset the scene.

### 2. 🛡️ City Defender (Campaign Wave Defense)
- **Wave 1: Oscorp Drone Patrol** - Intercept attacking spy drones before they scan the city.
- **Wave 2: Pumpkin Bomb Barrage** - Ticking pumpkin bombs drop from the sky. Your **Spider-Sense** tingling alarm will warn you—web and disarm them before detonation!
- **Wave 3: Vulture Air Raid** - Fast swooping dive-bombers slicing across the sky.
- **Wave 4: BOSS BATTLE - Green Goblin!** - Goblin zooms on his glider, laughing maniacally, firing cluster bombs. Web his glider to pull him down!
- **Wave 5: BOSS BATTLE - Doc Ock Tentacle Siege!** - Giant mechanical tentacles reach across the screen to crush buildings. Web up the glowing power cores!

### 3. 🎯 Target Frenzy (60-Second Arcade Rush)
- Rapid-fire reflex test: Gold (500 pts), Red (200 pts), Blue (100 pts), and Green Gliders (350 pts).
- Build up combo multipliers (up to 5x) for massive high scores saved locally!

### 4. 🚆 Train Rescue (Iconic Spider-Man 2 Two-Handed Stop)
- Runaway subway train rushing towards a broken bridge!
- Raise **BOTH hands** in the 🤟 Spider-Man pose to shoot dual web ropes to buildings on both sides!
- Pull and hold the tension as sparks fly from the tracks, rails creak, and the train screeches to a dramatic halt!
